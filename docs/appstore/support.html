Lumen RAW support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email theracinglineltd@gmail.com. I read every message. For a crash or a slow job, open the photo, tap ⋯ → Send diagnostics and attach the report: it has no photos in it.

Common questions

Which files open? Sony ARW, Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Fujifilm RAF, DNG, iPhone ProRAW, plus JPEG and HEIC.

What is free? Light, colour, crop, presets, and export without a watermark. Pro adds curves, grading, detail, masks, heal, AI Denoise, Lens Blur and HDR.

Restore a purchase: on the upgrade screen, tap Restore while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lumen RAW. Lifetime is a single payment and never renews.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
