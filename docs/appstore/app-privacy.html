Lumen RAW app privacy policy

Updated: 22 October 2026

Who is responsible: Angelo Parodi, independent developer. Contact theracinglineltd@gmail.com.

Your photos stay on your iPhone. Lumen RAW edits photos on the device. It keeps its copies of the photos you import, and your edits, in its own storage on the phone. It does not upload photos, and the developer has no server that receives them.

What the app collects: nothing. There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

Permissions: the app reads only the photos or files you choose to import, and saves to Photos only when you export and allow it. If you share an export to another app, that app's privacy policy applies.

Purchases: Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and tells the app whether Pro is active. The developer never sees your card or Apple Account details.

Diagnostics: if you tap Send diagnostics, the app prepares a text report (app version, memory and timing notes, recent errors; never photos) and you choose whether and where to send it.

Deleting data: removing a photo in the app deletes the app's copy and its edits. Your originals in Photos or Files are not touched. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

The early-access waitlist on this website is separate: see the waitlist privacy notice.
